One-line patch for the live marketing Privacy page (evorios.com/privacy.html).

The App Store seller for bundle `com.elflogistics.evorios` is ELF Logistics LLC.
Section 1 ("Who We Are") must name that same entity, or App Review will reject
the mismatch against the privacy URL.

FIND:
  Evorios LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company ("Evorios," "we," "us," "our"), operates the Evorios platform

REPLACE WITH:
  ELF Logistics LLC, a limited liability company doing business as Evorios ("Evorios," "we," "us," "our"), operates the Evorios platform

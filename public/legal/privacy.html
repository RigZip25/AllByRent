Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026 · Closed beta

We collect account details you provide (name, email, optional phone, location label), listing content and photos you upload, and transactional data needed for bookings and payouts.

Authentication and database hosting use Supabase. Payments use Stripe. Analytics or crash logs may be collected to keep the product reliable.

We do not sell your personal information. You can request account deletion from Profile → Delete account. Contact support@evorios.com for privacy requests.
